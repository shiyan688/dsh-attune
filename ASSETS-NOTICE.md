# Desktop companion artwork

The four images in `lib/assets/maid-*.webp` are the community's blue-haired whale maid, supplied by **SlimeNull · Chubby Whale / Chubby Whale contributors**:

- Project: <https://github.com/SlimeNull/ChubbyWhale>
- Community submission by SlimeNull: <https://github.com/TreapGoGo/deepseek-whale-girl/issues/80>
- Original files: `skins/standard/sprites/idle.webp`, `idle_blink.webp`, `happy.webp`, and `curious.webp`.
- Original art license: <https://github.com/SlimeNull/ChubbyWhale/blob/main/art/LICENSE.md>, preserved verbatim at `lib/assets/UPSTREAM-LICENSE.md`.

The character design refers to the community's **Q 版大肥鱼** model sheet in the [鲸鱼娘开源资产库](https://treapgogo.github.io/deepseek-whale-girl/). Character origin: **上善无形 / 上善**, original character 溟月; whale-maid adaptation incorporating DeepSeek elements: **ZipZipPipe**.
